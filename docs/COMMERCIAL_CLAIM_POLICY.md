# Commercial Claim Policy

M-Core Research OS is a proof-engineering research framework.

A commercial release may include:

- Lean source that builds under the pinned toolchain;
- formal definitions and proved lemmas;
- audit scripts and certificate infrastructure;
- conditional results;
- numerical or experimental evidence;
- explicitly OPEN_MATH terminal gaps.

A successful `lake build` establishes compilation/type-checking of the included Lean development. It must not be promoted as a solution of RH, P vs NP, Navier–Stokes, Yang–Mills, Hodge, BSD, Beal, or any other open problem unless the exact endpoint theorem is independently present and audited.

Historical notes, mining logs, and exploratory reports are provenance/research material, not theorem certificates.
