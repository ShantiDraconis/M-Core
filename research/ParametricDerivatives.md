# Parametric derivative identities

For real a and t>0 let s=1/2+a/t+it and w=(s-1/2)^2=a²/t²-t²+2ai. Differentiation gives s'=-a/t²+i, s''=2a/t³, s'''=-6a/t⁴; for n>=2, s^(n)=(-1)^n n! a/t^(n+1), by induction using the power rule. Also w'=-2a²/t³-2t, w''=6a²/t⁴-2, w'''=-24a²/t⁵. Im(w)=2a identically. For n>=3, w^(n)=(-1)^n (n+1)! a²/t^(n+2).

Let F=4t⁴/(4t⁴+a²), E=1-F. Quotient rule gives F'=16a²t³/(4t⁴+a²)^2 and F''=16a²t²(3a²-20t⁴)/(4t⁴+a²)^3. E'=-F', E''=-F''. Thus F is strictly increasing for a != 0, and tends to 1; E tends to 0. The inflection occurs at t⁴=3a²/20 for a != 0. These are unconditional elementary identities for arbitrary real a. They do not establish zeta zero locations or RH. Nominal parameter selection has no mathematical privilege.
