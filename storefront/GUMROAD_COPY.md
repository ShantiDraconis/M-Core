# M-Core Research OS

A Lean 4 proof-engineering research framework for formal audit, dependency control, certificate workflows, and explicit OPEN_MATH tracking.

## Included

- pinned Lean 4.19.0 / Mathlib 4.19.0 project
- formal definitions and verified Lean material
- audit and certificate infrastructure
- proof-chain documentation
- explicit status separation for PROVED, CONDITIONAL, NUMERICAL_EVIDENCE and OPEN
- release ZIP with SHA256 checksum

## Important research-status disclosure

M-Core Research OS does not market unresolved Millennium Problems or other OPEN_MATH endpoints as solved. A successful Lean build means the included formal development type-checks under the pinned environment; it is not by itself a proof of an external open conjecture.
